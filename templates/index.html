How the image is prepared

  1. Blur — a light Gaussian blur removes speckle before thresholding.
  2. Threshold — Otsu's method separates ink from background automatically, whatever the lighting.
  3. Crop — the largest contour is taken as the digit and cut to its bounding box.
  4. Scale — the digit is resized to fit a 20×20 box with its aspect ratio intact.
  5. Pad — it is centred inside a 28×28 frame, matching the MNIST convention.
